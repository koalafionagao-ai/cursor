Title: Agentic RL: Token-In, Token-Out Done Right (16 minute read)
Excerpt: In reinforcement learning with LLMs, ensuring the model operates on the exact tokens sampled is crucial. Re-tokenizing can lead to drift and unreliable gradients. The solution involves never re-encoding decoded tokens and maintaining a buffer for sampled tokens to avoid drift and maintain accurate loss computation. This approach depends on a prefix-preserving chat template property, which most modern templates satisfy, ensuring reliable reinforcement learning loops without redundant re-rendering.
